The story I hear most often is the same: "I'm doing everything I used to do. Why isn't it working anymore?"

The answer almost always comes back to hormones — and not the two everyone talks about. There are 10 different hormones involved in midlife weight loss, and most of them are never even tested. That's the gap I've spent my career closing.

My approach isn't anti-medication. In severe cases, HRT can be life-changing. Many of the symptoms — the anxiety, the broken sleep, the bloating, the weight gain — are driven by the loss of progesterone, not just the drop in estrogen. Address those naturally first, and many women find significant relief without medication. Or they find a much lower dose of HRT works perfectly.

If you've been working at this for years and the scale won't move, it's not you. It's a hormone signaling problem. And every single one of these 10 hormones can be supported naturally and holistically — when you know which ones are out of sync.
